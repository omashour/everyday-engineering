# Assets needed

## checkout.jpg (used in S01 and thumbnail variant 2)
- What: a supermarket checkout lane in landscape format, with the cashier in the left third and a line of 3–5 shoppers going off to the right. Shoppers seen from behind or from the side are best, so faces aren't the focus.
- Layout the overlays expect: cashier at about 20% across and 35% down; the line of shoppers filling roughly 40–95% across and 30–85% down; the top 15% plain (a title banner covers it).
- AI prompt (Gemini or ChatGPT; ask for a wide 16:9 image):

```text
Photorealistic wide 16:9 photo of a busy supermarket checkout lane, eye-level view, natural store lighting, slightly warm tones. A cashier in a plain uniform works at the register in the left third of the frame, scanning groceries on the conveyor belt, seen from a three-quarter side angle. A line of four or five shoppers with carts and baskets stretches from the register toward the right edge of the frame, seen mostly from behind or from the side so no face is the focus. The upper part of the frame is plain ceiling and soft lights. Realistic everyday scene, sharp focus, shallow depth of field on the background shelves. No text, no signs with words, no logos or brand names, no watermarks.
```

- If you use the AI image: set `credit` in S01 to "Image: AI-generated". Check YouTube's altered-or-synthetic-content guidance when you upload.
- Real photo instead: Wikimedia Commons (search "supermarket checkout queue" or "checkout line") or Openverse (filter: commercial use allowed), or your own photo. License CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA; put the author and license in `credit`.
- If the cashier and line end up in different places in your image, tell Claude and it will move the S01 overlays.
