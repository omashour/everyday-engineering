**Title:** The Numbers on Your Tire Are a Math Problem
**Alternates:** Change One Digit on Your Tire and Your Speedometer Lies · What 225/45R17 Actually Means (With the Math)
**Playlist:** math-for-engineers
**Description:** Every tire has a size code like 225/45R17 on its sidewall. It isn't a part number. It's a small math problem, and solving it gives you the tire's exact diameter and shows why changing one digit makes your speedometer read wrong.

We read each part of the code, turn the aspect ratio into a sidewall height and add up the overall diameter: 634 mm, about 25 inches. Then we use the circumference to see how your speedometer and odometer measure speed and distance. Change 45 to 55 and the speedometer reads about 7% low. Plus sizing is how bigger wheels keep the speedometer accurate.

Assumptions: nominal tire sizes (new tread, no load). Under the car's weight the real rolling circumference is slightly smaller.
