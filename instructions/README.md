## How the pieces fit

```
Claude.ai (skill)            Gemini / ChatGPT                Everyday Engineering app (this PC)
-----------------            ----------------                ----------------------------------
suggest topics  ──► you pick
suggest styles  ──► you pick
write the script ─────────────────────────────────────────►  paste it in "Script from Claude"
                              make images from the prompts ►  drop them in "Photos"
                              record the voice chunks ─────►  drop them in "Voice"
                                                              "Make everything" → video, Shorts,
                                                              thumbnails, upload sheet
YouTube ◄──────────────────────────────────────────────────── copy each field, upload, "Mark as published"
```

- **Claude.ai** does the creative writing (topics, script, titles, image prompts).
- **Gemini** (Google AI Studio) records the narration; **Gemini or ChatGPT** makes images.
- **The app** on your PC does everything else for free: timing, building, rendering, Shorts, thumbnails and the upload sheet.
- **GitHub** keeps the plan, scripts and topic list in sync between PCs and with Claude.ai. Videos and recordings stay on your PC (see guide 7).
