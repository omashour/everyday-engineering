# Roadmap

The channel grows in phases. Each phase builds an audience that the next one can keep.

| Phase | Content | Main styles | Rough size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Math for engineers *(now)* | Math taught through the everyday engineering it powers | B hooks, A, C | 30–50 videos |
| 2. Physics and chemistry | Mechanics, energy, electricity, heat, materials, reactions, always with an engineering use | B, A, C, D | 30–50 videos |
| 3. Engineering fundamentals | Solved problems: statics, mechanics of materials, dynamics, circuits, fluids, thermodynamics, engineering economics | F, A, C | numbered series |
| 4. Industrial engineering | Optimization, linear programming, queueing, inventory, reliability, quality/Six Sigma, supply chains, simulation | F, C, D | numbered series |

Rules of thumb:
- About 1 in 3 videos should be broad-appeal "why the world works this way" stories (styles B/D). These are the most likely to spread.
- Every math topic marked `leads_to` in the backlog should later get a matching engineering problem video that links back to it.
- After every 10 published videos, check YouTube Analytics and move the best-performing subjects up the backlog.
