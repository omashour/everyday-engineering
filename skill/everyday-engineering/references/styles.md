# Styles

Generated by `npm run docs` from styles/styles.yaml. Suggest from A, B, C, D, F (E = Shorts, always produced).

## A · Whiteboard Build
White background, equations built line by line, yellow marker, red circles/arrows/notes, simple diagrams. Closest to Brain Station; cheapest.
- Best for: derivations; worked examples; algebra; trigonometry; logarithms; probability calculations; how to solve any X
- Avoid for: stories; things that move over time
- Components: `diagram`, `equation-steps`, `table`

## B · Real-World Overlay
Photo of the real object with animated measurements, arrows, callouts and math on top. The channel's signature hook.
- Best for: hooks (first 15 s of any video); everyday objects; structures; roads; products
- Avoid for: long derivations
- Needs: a photo per scene (own or CC0/CC BY with credit)
- Components: `photo-overlay`

## C · Graph Lab
Animated graphs - curves drawing themselves, shaded areas, moving points, reference lines.
- Best for: calculus; growth and decay; waves; vibration; differential equations; queueing curves; anything that changes over time
- Avoid for: pure symbol manipulation
- Components: `graph`

## D · Story / Case Study
Timeline, archive-style photos, quotes, then the math that explains the event.
- Best for: real events; disasters; famous engineers; why the world works this way; viral-style videos
- Avoid for: routine exercises
- Needs: accurate dates and sources; archive photos with licenses
- Components: `quote`, `timeline`

## E · Shorts
Vertical 9:16 cut of the long video - hook banner, 2-3 scenes, end card. Built automatically from shorts.yaml.
- Best for: every long video gets 1-2
- Components: `short-end`

## F · Engineering Problem Solver
Textbook layout - problem card (figure, Given, Find, Assume), numbered steps with units, boxed answer, sanity check, optional pause-and-try, practice problem.
- Best for: solved problems in statics; mechanics of materials; circuits; fluids; thermodynamics; engineering economics; industrial engineering (LP; queueing; inventory; reliability; quality)
- Avoid for: broad-appeal hooks
- Components: `answer-box`, `beam`, `pause-try`, `problem-card`

Shared components usable in any style: `big-fact`, `bullets`, `end-card`, `image`, `title-card`.
